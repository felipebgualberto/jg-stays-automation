Where the numbers come from. Prices: nightly rate asked by listings of the same type nearby (PriceLabs market data), discounted 10% to reach the rate actually sold — the gap we see in our own units. Occupancy: real market occupancy month by month over the last 12 months. Seasonality: median price of each of the next 12 months.

Scenarios. Conservative: 1st-quartile rate, occupancy 10 points below market. Moderate: median rate, market occupancy. Managed by JG Stays: median rate, occupancy 8 points above market — below what our units delivered in the last 90 days.

Deductions. Platform fees 18% of what the guest pays (average of our Airbnb + Booking bookings), PMS 1.9%, cleaning passed to the cleaning team, 20% management commission. An estimate, not a revenue guarantee.
